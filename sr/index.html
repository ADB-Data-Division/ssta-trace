Super-resolution service · no coding needed

Make an image

Choose a place and a date. The service finds clear Sentinel-2 images and makes a super-resolved image with 2.5 m pixels, which you can look at here, put on a slide, or open in QGIS or a Python notebook.

Connect with your email address

Your address goes only to the image service, which gives you a personal key (the same address always gets the same key). Nothing is sent to your inbox. If you were given a key that starts with srk_, type it instead.
