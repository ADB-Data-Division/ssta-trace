How to read the entries

SR fit uses a size rule: super-resolution (SR) helped in our tests where the things to map are 2.5 to 10 m wide. It is our judgement, not a measurement.

LikelyThe things mapped are 2.5 to 10 m wide. This is where SR helped in our tests.
MaybeSR may help with the small or narrow part (thin strips, small plots, edges). Test it.
UnlikelyThe things are 10 m and more, or under water. Free Sentinel-2 should do as well. Good as a control: does SR make anything worse?
ContextNot a test of SR. It helps you choose places and dates, or weigh a decision by people or money.

Labels by people Most open maps are made by a model, often from Sentinel-2 itself. They are a second opinion, or the 10 m result that SR has to beat. They are not the truth. Entries with this mark hold labels that people drew on sharper images or collected on the ground. Prefer them when you can.

Non-commercial The licence allows non-commercial use only. Check the licence on the dataset's page before you use it in your office.

Get it. "EE" means Google Earth Engine (free account needed), with the name of the layer. Sizes are for the whole download; most global maps come in tiles, so you only take the tile for your area.

Dates. SR needs Sentinel-2 images, so it works from about 2017. Ask for SR images of the year the dataset describes, not of today.

How this list was checked

  • Five research agents (Claude Opus) searched one theme each. Each one had to open every page it cites and note what it saw there.
  • Every link on this page was then opened again by a separate script (131 links, 26 November 2026). 124 answered. The other 7 are on three sites that refuse scripts but open in a browser: the Humanitarian Data Exchange, figshare and Harvard Dataverse. For those we confirmed the dataset, its title and its licence in the site's own data service or in the DOI register.
  • For the datasets on Zenodo, the title, licence and size were compared with Zenodo's own records. Every Earth Engine layer name was found on the page that documents it.
  • What was not checked: we did not download every file, and we did not test each dataset against SR. "SR fit" is a judgement from the size of the things mapped.

Left out on purpose: datasets whose pictures have lost their coordinates (they cannot be matched with Sentinel-2), datasets that end before 2016, and datasets we could not download. Tell us if a link stops working.
